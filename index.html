02 / How I work

From reporting pressure to a controlled workflow.

Framework knowledge matters. Delivery discipline is what makes it usable.

  1. 01

    Map the obligation

    Translate frameworks and stakeholder expectations into a defined disclosure and action scope.

  2. 02

    Find the evidence

    Connect each claim and KPI to an owner, source, method, reporting period, and confidence level.

  3. 03

    Build the cadence

    Create trackers, decision points, escalation paths, and review cycles that make delivery repeatable.

    Make it usable

    Present the result in a tool or narrative that leadership, operations, and assurance teams can act on.
